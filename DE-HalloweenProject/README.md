This is a circuit that I made for my Digital Electronics class. It is designed to play a very simplified version of spooky scary skeletons.

Circuit Description: The circuit will consist of a 6Hz clock driving a 5-bit binary counter. LEDs will be connected to each output of the binary counter and one will be connected to the 6Hz clock. Logic gates will be connected to the output of the binary counter, and will activate their corresponding note-timer when the binary counter reaches the number that the timer should activate at. The musical notes will be G4, F4, C4, D4, and B3. The notes will be played on a speaker. The circuit will be powered though 4 1.5V batteries and a 5V voltage regulator. A simple switch will serve as the on/off mechanism.

In this folder, there are the final photos/videos of the project, the notesheet that I used as a reference, and the target audio generated by Online Sequencer.
